MOP-1. Seller Performance Tiers

1.1 Every active seller is assigned a performance tier each calendar month, calculated on the trailing 90 days of completed orders. Sellers with fewer than 15 completed orders in that window are classified Unrated and are exempt from tier-based penalties, though not from Section 6.

1.2 Tiers are determined by two measures: on-time delivery rate (OTD) and adverse review rate (ARR, the share of orders receiving 1 or 2 stars).
Tier | OTD | ARR
Gold | ≥ 95% | ≤ 4%
Silver | ≥ 88% | ≤ 9%
Bronze | ≥ 75% | ≤ 15%
Watch | below Bronze on either measure | —

1.3 A seller must satisfy both thresholds for a tier. A seller with 96% OTD and 11% ARR is Bronze, not Gold.

1.4 Tier changes take effect on the first business day of the month and are communicated via the Seller Portal no fewer than 5 business days in advance. Demotion to Watch is an exception and takes effect immediately on calculation.

1.5 Gold sellers receive priority placement in category listings, a reduced commission rate of 11% against the standard 14%, and access to the Rapid Payout schedule described in MOP-7.

MOP-2. Delivery Delay Classification

2.1 Delay is measured against the estimated delivery date shown to the customer at checkout, not against the carrier’s own estimate.

2.2 Delays are classified as follows:
Class | Delay | Seller liability
Minor | 1–3 days | None
Moderate | 4–10 days | Freight cost charged back to seller
Severe | 11–20 days | Freight chargeback plus 5% order value penalty
Critical | over 20 days, or undelivered at 30 days | Full order value charged back; order auto-cancelled

2.3 Where a delay is attributable to a documented carrier failure, a declared force majeure event, or an address error made by the customer, seller liability under 2.2 is waived. The seller must raise a Liability Waiver Request within 10 calendar days of delivery or of the auto-cancellation date.

2.4 Delays on orders shipped to the North and Northeast regions receive an automatic 4-day grace allowance before classification, reflecting known logistics constraints. This allowance does not apply to orders in the Express Freight programme.

MOP-3. Customer Compensation

3.1 Compensation is issued as platform credit by default. Cash refunds to the original payment method are available only under MOP-4.

3.2 Standard compensation for delivery delay, issued automatically:
Delay class | Credit
Minor | None
Moderate | R$ 20 or 10% of order value, whichever is greater
Severe | R$ 50 or 20% of order value, whichever is greater
Critical | 100% of order value plus freight

3.3 Compensation above R$ 300 requires approval from a Quality Team lead. Compensation above R$ 1,000 requires approval from the Head of Marketplace Operations and must be recorded in the Exceptions Register.

3.4 A customer may receive no more than three discretionary compensations in any rolling 12-month period. Automatic compensation under 3.2 does not count toward this limit.

3.5 Compensation for reasons other than delivery delay — product defect, item not as described, missing items — follows the Returns Policy and is not covered by this document.

MOP-4. Cash Refund Eligibility

4.1 A cash refund to the original payment method may be issued where any one of the following holds:
• The order is classified Critical under MOP-2
• The order was cancelled before carrier handoff
• The customer has an open consumer protection complaint registered with Procon
• The customer account has been closed and platform credit cannot be applied

4.2 Cash refunds are processed within 10 business days for credit card payments and within 3 business days for Pix and boleto payments.

4.3 Partial cash refunds are not supported. Where a partial remedy is appropriate, platform credit must be used.

MOP-5. Review Moderation

5.1 Reviews may be removed only where they contain personal data, abusive language, content unrelated to the order, or evidence of manipulation.

5.2 A negative review may not be removed on the grounds that the seller disputes it, that compensation was paid, or that the cause was a carrier failure. Where a review reflects a delay for which seller liability was waived under MOP-2.3, the review is excluded from the seller’s ARR calculation but remains visible on the listing.

5.3 Sellers may post one public response per review. Responses are subject to the same content standards and may not reference the customer by name.

5.4 Removal requests must be raised within 30 days of review posting. The moderation decision is final and there is no second-level appeal.

MOP-6. Seller Escalation and Suspension

6.1 A seller entering Watch tier is placed in a 30-day Improvement Period and assigned an operations contact. Listings remain active.

6.2 A seller who remains in Watch tier at the end of the Improvement Period is subject to Listing Restriction: existing listings remain, new listings are blocked, and category placement is deprioritised.

6.3 Suspension may be applied where a seller remains under Listing Restriction for 60 days, or immediately where there is evidence of counterfeit goods, review manipulation, or failure to ship more than 25% of orders in any 30-day window.

6.4 Before any suspension, the Quality Team must complete and record all of the following:
1. Written notice to the seller with at least 10 business days to respond, except where 6.3 immediate grounds apply
2. A review of open Liability Waiver Requests, which must be resolved first
3. Confirmation that outstanding customer orders have a fulfilment or refund plan
4. Sign-off from a Quality Team lead

6.5 Suspended sellers may apply for reinstatement after 90 days. Sellers suspended for counterfeit goods are permanently removed and may not reapply.

MOP-7. Payouts

7.1 Standard payout is 30 days after confirmed delivery. Rapid Payout, available to Gold tier sellers, is 7 days after confirmed delivery.

7.2 Payouts are withheld in full for sellers under Listing Restriction or suspension, and released on resolution less any chargebacks under MOP-2.

7.3 Where a seller’s accumulated chargebacks exceed their pending payout balance, the deficit is carried forward. A deficit outstanding for more than 120 days is referred for collection.

MOP-8. Freight Cost Allocation

8.1 Freight is quoted at checkout based on declared product weight and dimensions and on the distance between seller and customer postcodes.

8.2 Where actual weight or dimensions exceed declared values by more than 15%, the difference in freight cost is charged to the seller.

8.3 Freight on returns is borne by the seller where the return results from a defect, a misshipment, or an item not as described, and by the customer where the return is a change of mind under the statutory withdrawal right.

8.4 The Express Freight programme carries a surcharge paid by the customer and a tightened SLA. Express orders delayed beyond 2 days are classified Moderate at minimum, regardless of the thresholds in MOP-2.2, and the regional grace allowance in MOP-2.4 does not apply.
